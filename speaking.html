Topic of the Day

"The Power of Failure"

Talk about a time you failed and what you learned from it. Focus on the growth, not the mistake.

💡 Speaking Tips

  • Start with a strong opening — a question or a bold statement
  • Use the "3-point structure": problem, solution, takeaway
  • Make eye contact (even if practicing alone, look at a mirror)
  • Pause between key points — it shows confidence
  • End with a memorable closing line or call to action
